ACCOUNTING TOPICS COVERED

Our Accounting course is meticulously designed for coaching students to develop a thorough and clear understanding of accounting principles and practices. Each topic is explained in detail, ensuring that students not only memorize concepts but also comprehend their practical applications in real business scenarios. The content is structured to gradually build knowledge from basic to advanced topics, making learning easier and more effective.

Introduction to Accounting

This topic introduces students to the meaning, nature, and purpose of accounting. Students learn how accounting serves as a systematic process to record, classify, and summarize financial transactions, and how it provides critical information for business decision-making and financial planning.

Basic Accounting Concepts & Principles

Students explore fundamental accounting concepts and principles such as the business entity, going concern, money measurement, consistency, prudence, and accrual concept. Understanding these principles helps students record transactions accurately, maintain reliability, and ensure consistency in financial reporting.

Journal Entries

This section focuses on the method of recording business transactions using the rules of debit and credit. Students practice various types of journal entries, including compound entries, adjustments, and correction entries, which form the foundation for all accounting processes.

Ledger Accounts

Students learn the process of posting journal entries into ledger accounts and how to prepare and balance these accounts. This topic emphasizes accuracy and organization, which are crucial for maintaining clear financial records and preparing trial balances.

Trial Balance

This topic covers the preparation, purpose, and importance of the trial balance. Students understand how the trial balance serves as a tool to detect errors, ensure that debit and credit balances match, and prepare for the creation of financial statements.
