ALGEBRA TOPICS COVERED

Our Algebra course is designed to build strong problem-solving and logical thinking skills required in mathematics, commerce, and higher studies. Each topic is explained with slightly more detailed and balanced text than basic outlines, ensuring uniformity across all headings and a clear understanding for students and parents.

Introduction to Algebra

This topic introduces the basic meaning and importance of algebra. Students learn how algebra uses symbols and variables to represent numbers and solve mathematical problems logically.

Algebraic Expressions

Students study algebraic expressions involving variables, constants, and coefficients. This topic explains how to form, simplify, and evaluate expressions used in various calculations.

Polynomials

This section covers polynomials and their types such as monomials, binomials, and trinomials. Students learn operations on polynomials including addition, subtraction, and multiplication.

Linear Equations

Students learn how to form and solve linear equations in one and two variables. This topic helps develop step-by-step problem-solving skills and logical reasoning.

Quadratic Equations

This topic introduces quadratic equations and their methods of solution. Students learn factorization and formula-based approaches with practical examples.
