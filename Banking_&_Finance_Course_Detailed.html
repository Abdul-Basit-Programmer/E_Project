BANKING & FINANCE TOPICS COVERED

Our Banking & Finance course is designed to give students a clear, practical, and in-depth understanding of banking systems and financial concepts. Each topic is explained with slightly more detailed and balanced text than basic outlines, ensuring uniformity across all headings and helping students and parents clearly understand what will be taught.

Introduction to Banking & Finance

This topic introduces the meaning and scope of banking and finance. Students learn how banking and financial systems support businesses, individuals, and overall economic development.

Banking System

Students study the structure of the banking system and its role in the economy. This topic explains how banks operate and provide financial stability.

Types of Banks

This section covers different types of banks such as commercial banks, central banks, and specialized banks. Students learn their functions, roles, and importance in the financial system.

Functions of Banks

Students learn the primary and secondary functions of banks including accepting deposits, providing loans, and offering agency services. Practical examples help explain banking operations.

Central Banking System

This topic focuses on the role and functions of the central bank. Students learn about currency issuance, credit control, and regulation of the banking system.
