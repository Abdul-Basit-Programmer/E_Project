PROBABILITY TOPICS COVERED

Our Probability course is designed to help students understand uncertainty, chance, and prediction in a clear and logical manner. Each topic is explained with slightly more detailed and balanced text than earlier outlines, maintaining uniformity across all headings and helping students apply probability concepts confidently in academics and real-life situations.

Introduction to Probability

This topic introduces the basic meaning and importance of probability. Students learn how probability measures the likelihood of events and why it is useful in decision-making and analysis.

Random Experiments

Students study the concept of random experiments and outcomes. This topic explains how experiments lead to different possible results that form the foundation of probability.

Sample Space

This section covers the idea of sample space and listing all possible outcomes of an experiment. Students learn systematic methods to represent outcomes clearly.

Events

Students learn what events are in probability and how they relate to sample space. This topic explains simple and compound events with clear examples.

Classical Probability

This topic explains the classical definition of probability. Students learn how to calculate probability when all outcomes are equally likely.
