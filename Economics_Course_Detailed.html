ECONOMICS TOPICS COVERED

Our Economics course is designed to help students develop a clear and in-depth understanding of economic concepts and their real-life applications. Each topic is explained with equal detail and balanced text to maintain uniformity, making it easy for students and parents to understand the complete syllabus.

Introduction to Economic

This topic introduces students to the meaning, nature, and scope of economics. Students learn how economics studies human behavior related to limited resources and unlimited wants.

Basic Economic Concepts

Students learn fundamental economic concepts such as scarcity, choice, opportunity cost, goods, services, and factors of production. These concepts form the base for understanding advanced economic topics.

Microeconomics

This section focuses on individual economic units such as consumers and firms. Students study demand, supply, laws of demand and supply, and price determination in markets.

Consumer Behavior

Students learn how consumers make choices and decisions. This topic explains utility, satisfaction, and consumer equilibrium with simple explanations and examples.

Production and Cost

This topic explains the process of production and the role of factors of production. Students learn different types of costs and their impact on production decisions.
